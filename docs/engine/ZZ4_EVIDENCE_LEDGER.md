Research date: **2026-10-07 UTC**. Target requirement: [Issue #22](https://github.com/coantoai/NAHLATY_MY_BEE/issues/22), Chevrolet Performance ZZ4 350 V8 represented by a single-cylinder explanatory cutaway.

**The factory evidence lock remains incomplete, but primary Chevrolet specifications have now been retrieved and inspected.** The reference is the documented **ZZ4 Engine (24502609 Base) Long Block**, specifications part number **19172321**, revision **09JA15 (2015-01-09)**. Verified specifications below apply to that documented configuration. A plausible conversion, a renderer constant, and a passing motion test do not independently establish an as-built ZZ4 specification.

## Classification rules

- **VERIFIED** means the cited evidence directly supports the particular claim and its scope. The requirement source and inspected Git implementation are available evidence; neither is a factory specification.
- **INFERRED** means a calculation, general engineering model, or visual choice is explicitly conditional on stated inputs or assumptions. It must not be presented as measured or documented ZZ4 geometry/timing.
- **UNKNOWN** means the required factory support is absent or inaccessible. A candidate value can be retained for review without being accepted as a factory fact.

Evidence strength is separate from status: requirement-only evidence identifies what was requested; implementation-only evidence identifies what code does; conditional mathematical evidence checks a consequence of inputs; direct factory evidence identifies the documented engine specification. No guessed dimensions, timing angles, document titles, part numbers, revisions, or page numbers are supplied.
